# StudyFlow v20 — Study & Goals + Background Focus Timer

- Kept the Home screen free of separate Upcoming and Grades cards.
- Restyled Study & Goals using the earlier clean white-card visual language.
- Added compact white summary cards for Study Progress and Active Goals.
- Added a white Study & Goals panel with progress bar, active goals, completed goals, and Focus action.
- Focus Timer now keeps its end time in persistent storage so it continues while StudyFlow is backgrounded or another app is open.
- Schedules a native Android completion notification for the Focus Timer when the app is not in the foreground.
- Reconciles the countdown from the real end timestamp when StudyFlow is reopened/resumed.
- Reset cancels the pending focus notification.
- Goal/event completion checkmarks are now SVG-based and centered inside their circles.
- Preserved the customizable Focus Timer with seconds, minutes, and hours.
