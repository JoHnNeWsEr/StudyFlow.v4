# StudyFlow v23

## Live Focus + Home Events
- Live Focus countdown remains beside the exact goal/task/event being focused inside StudyFlow.
- Added a native Android ongoing Focus notification with a real countdown in the phone notification shade while another app is open or StudyFlow is minimized/closed.
- The notification identifies the focused task/goal and counts down independently of the WebView.
- The existing scheduled completion notification remains in place for the end of the session.
- Stopping/resetting a Focus session removes the live ongoing notification.
- Restored Upcoming Events to the Home screen, with the same completion and Focus controls used on the Events page.
- Tutorial continues to launch at app startup and remains replayable from Settings.
- Grades, Academic Calendar and the scanner remain removed.
