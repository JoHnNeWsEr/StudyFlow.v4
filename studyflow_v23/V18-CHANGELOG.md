# StudyFlow v18 — Full Study Toolkit

Built on the scanner-free v17 release.

- Assignments, quizzes, exams, oral recitations and projects remain in Events.

- Added Semester management for semester/school-year labels.
- Improved Home dashboard with upcoming, goal and grade stats.
- Existing reminders/notifications, backup/restore, theme and profile photo remain.
